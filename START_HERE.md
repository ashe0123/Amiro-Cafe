## 💡 Common Questions

### Q: Do I need a database?
**A:** No! Everything works with browser storage. Perfect for getting started.

### Q: Does it cost money to run?
**A:** No! You can deploy for free on Vercel or Netlify.

### Q: Can I customize it?
**A:** Yes! Everything can be changed - colors, content, images, etc.

### Q: Is it mobile-friendly?
**A:** Yes! It's specifically designed for mobile QR code scanning.

### Q: Can I add my own menu items?
**A:** Yes! Use the admin panel to add/edit/delete items.

### Q: How do I get my QR code?
**A:** Go to Admin → QR Code → Download
